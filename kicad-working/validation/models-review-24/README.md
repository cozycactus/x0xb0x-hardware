# KiCad working revision 24 — printed IC markings

The 17 ICs now carry printed part markings transcribed from the original kit photographs on the parts page (archived at x0xb0x.github.io; photographs not stored here, hashes in `marking-quality/sources/marking-evidence.json`): P0418SB / MM74HC595N on IC4-IC8, P0436SB / 74AC174PC on IC9, P0412SB / MM74HC126N on IC1, MC14066BCP / FFGY9338 on IC12, 6562 / 4 91 on IC11 and IC23, ATMEGA162 / 16PI / 0437F on IC3 (across the body, as photographed), 25C33P / 0220A on IC2, L0434 / 4N37 on IC24 and FT232BM / 425-1 on IC25. The 74HC165s (IC16-IC18) have no photograph and carry 74HC165N only. Logos are omitted; date and lot codes come from one photographed kit.

`marking-quality/build_markings.py` keeps each chip's current model (the KiCad DIP/LQFP STEP, or the revision-23 socketed IC3/IC2 model) with its solids and per-face colours unchanged and adds glyph solids 0.03 mm proud of the flat chip top in light-grey ink, set in Source Code Pro Medium (open font bundled with FreeCAD; it approximates the vendors' marking fonts). DIP text reads from the notch end with the pin-1 row at the bottom; the FT232BM reads with its pin-1 dimple at the top left. It passes 60 placement, fit, reading-direction, colour-preservation and round-trip checks.

`install_marking_models.py` changes 17 PCB model paths and six single-placement footprint caches; the shared DIL16/DIL14 caches keep the unmarked KiCad body. PCB value fields (e.g. IC9 4174P, IC1 74126N, IC2 AT25P) are unchanged. Fresh KiCad 10.0.7 ERC reports 0 errors/0 warnings; DRC violations, parity and ignored checks equal revision 23. The native audit finds a lead in every drilled hole of all 491 models with the 11 known findings unchanged, and `marking-quality/check_native_markings.py` confirms ink on every chip top with all other colours unchanged. See `../working-validation-summary-revision-24.json`.
