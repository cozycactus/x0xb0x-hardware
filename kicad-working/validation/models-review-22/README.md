# KiCad working revision 22 — original-kit resistor colour bands

All 212 fixed resistors now carry the colour bands of the parts supplied in the original kit. The original parts page (archived mirror in `visualization/blender-case-revision-7/bom-parts/`) prints the bands for every value; `resistor-quality/extract_resistor_bands.py` reads them and checks that each printed band list decodes to its row's value. 144 references are listed by row; the 10K row's printed list stops after 26 of its 54 references, and the remaining 28 PCB references are all 10K; the 40 LED resistors ("value may vary") take their PCB value, 10K. The 200K 1% row prints no bands; its photograph reads Red Black Black Orange Brown. The 4.7K row's "GOld" is read as Gold.

The 20 1% metal-film parts (R4, R5, R106, R74-R90) get a light-blue body sampled from the four parts-page photographs and five bands; the 192 5% parts get the tan body of the page's band graphic (no photograph exists) and four bands. R16 and R106 are banded per the parts page (150 ohm, 24.0K 1%); their PCB values (100, 27K) are unchanged.

`resistor-quality/build_resistors.py` splits each installed KiCad DIN0207 STEP, without moving or reshaping it, into body, two leads and flush band rings cut from the body's own material, so the outline is the KiCad outline. It passes 376 partition, envelope, flush-surface, reading-order and round-trip checks over 31 footprint/band variants. Upright parts read from the top, lying parts from the pad-1 end; band widths, positions and colour values are illustrations.

`install_resistor_models.py` changes only the 212 model path strings in the two PCB files; the shared 0207 footprint caches keep the unbanded KiCad body. Fresh KiCad 10.0.7 ERC reports 0 errors/0 warnings; DRC violations, parity and ignored checks equal revision 21. The native audit finds a lead in every drilled hole of all 491 models with the 11 known findings unchanged, and `resistor-quality/check_native_bands.py` reads the assigned bands back from the native export for all 212 resistors. See `../working-validation-summary-revision-22.json`. Physical assembly remains unqualified.
