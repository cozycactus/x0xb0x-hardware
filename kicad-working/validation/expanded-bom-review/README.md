# Expanded BOM cross-check

The AdaWiki expanded bill of materials (`x0x:billofmaterials`, last modified 2016-01-28) blocks automated fetches, so revisions 21-24 relied on search-engine extracts of it. The repository owner printed the page to PDF in a browser on 2026-10-16; the print is kept byte-for-byte in `sources/`. `crosscheck_expanded_bom.py` extracts its text with Poppler's `pdftotext` and writes `../../../docs/expanded-bom-crosscheck.json` (22 checks); it changes no model.

- **Electrolytics:** all seven Xicon XRL part numbers are confirmed and the eight rows name exactly the 39 electrolytics modelled in revision 21. The two 100uF Mouser numbers are transposed relative to their descriptions (10V row: 140-xrl25v100; 25V row: 140-xrl10v100). The descriptions are right: C7/C8 hang off the rail C5 stacks on the rectified input (well above 10 V), while C48/C49/C102 sit on +6 V/+5 V. Revision-21 sizes stand.
- **Resistors:** 208 of the 209 listed resistors match the revision-22 bands, including R106 at 24K 1% (PCB value 27K). R157, R158 and R160 are unlisted but are 10K on the parts page and PCB and fill the 10K row's stated quantity. R16 is 100 ohm here and on the PCB; the parts page gives 100 ohm for older kits and 150 ohm for newer kits, and revision 22 shows 150 ohm. Revision 22's description of R16 as a PCB/parts-page difference is corrected here.
- **Sockets and ICs:** socket (571-3902625/-3902612 = AMP 390262-5/390261-2), EEPROM (cat25c33p), 4N37 and FT232BM rows agree with revisions 23-24. The page names IC16-IC18 74AC165 (the revision-24 marking shows 74HC165N, which the parts page also allows) and the microcontroller Atmega162-40PI (the photographed part reads 16PI).
