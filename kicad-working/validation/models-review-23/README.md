# KiCad working revision 23 — original-BOM IC sockets

IC3 (ATmega162-16PU) and IC2 (CAT2532 EEPROM) now sit in the sockets the original parts page lists: AMP 390262-5 (40-pin, IC3') and AMP 390261-2 (8-pin, IC2*). TE publishes manufacturer 3D models (Customer View Models) only for the lead-free successors 1-390262-5 (2009) and 1-390261-2 (2012); both archives are kept byte-for-byte in `socket-quality/sources/` and identical geometry to the BOM part numbers is assumed. They match the distributor data (5.10 mm above the board, 3.00 mm posts) and the kit photograph (black open-frame ladder socket, tin contacts). TE's customer drawings could not be retrieved.

`socket-quality/build_sockets.py` builds one STEP per IC in the unchanged KiCad DIP model frame: the TE socket, geometry untouched, turned so its notch is at the chip's pin-1 end and its tails lie on the chip pin axes, seated on the board; plus the installed KiCad DIP chip raised 5.10 mm so its lead shoulders rest on the socket top (an assumption). The chip keeps its KiCad face colours; socket contact faces reaching below the board are tin and the housing is black. It passes 30 checks, including tails on every pad axis and 0.17 x 0.60 mm flat tails whose 0.62 mm diagonal fits the original 0.8128 mm drills (0.094 mm nominal corner margin).

`install_socket_models.py` changes four model path strings: IC3 and IC2 in the PCB and their single-placement footprint caches DIL40 and DIL08_AT25. Fresh KiCad 10.0.7 ERC reports 0 errors/0 warnings; DRC violations, parity and ignored checks equal revision 22. The native audit finds a lead in every drilled hole of all 491 models with the 11 known findings unchanged; `socket-quality/check_native_sockets.py` confirms the housing height, tin contacts and the chips at 8.78 mm in the native export. See `../working-validation-summary-revision-23.json`. Physical assembly remains unqualified.
