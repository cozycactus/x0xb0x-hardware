# Original panel visual evidence

The original Ladyada panel photograph establishes the cap colors unambiguously:
**S17–S24 are white; S5–S16 and S25–S27 are black.** That is 8 white and 15 black.
The current delivered scene already uses this exact assignment.

`original-panel-visual-evidence.json` supplies all 23 reference mappings,
source-derived label facts, panel centres and source hashes. The original
project page and finishing instructions are retained at the same pinned
Git commit used for the mechanical DXFs. Their Git blob hashes were verified.

The full original public panel image is 1499 × 1114 pixels. Its Flickr page
explicitly says **All rights reserved**; it is retained as research reference,
not proposed as a texture or redistributable panel-art asset. The original
MIT design license does not establish a license for this photograph.

The original panel marks the waveform switch **SAW/SQR**. Its bank selector
has a numbered 1–16 ring, and its mode selector has 16 specific legends. The
photograph has no x0xb0x title or SYNTHESIZER / SEQUENCER subtitle in the area
where the current scene adds those presentation labels. The JSON records
these differences without editing the delivered model.

The pinned mirror and original download page provide mechanical DXFs but no
editable printed artwork. The public AdaWiki page links converted bare AI
files and community modified art. Both bare AI links returned HTTP403;
the community art is not the original Adafruit panel. No replacement
silkscreen coordinates, typography or traced art were invented.

Delivered Blender and PCB files were left unchanged.
