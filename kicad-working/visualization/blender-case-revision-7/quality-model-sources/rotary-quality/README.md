# Detailed rotary and toggle review candidates

Three combined colored STEP/STL families cover S2 (CTS 290VAA5F201B2), S1 (NKK B12AB), and S3/S4 (Grayhill 25LB22-H). The assets are frozen for review and integration. Source PCB files, source models and delivered scenes remain unchanged by generation and isolated native probes.

The canonical frame remains original F.Cu footprint X, minus footprint Y, and positive Z outward from the component seat. Assignment offsets and rotations are zero, scale is one; actual side/position/angle are applied once by KiCad or the scene builder.

- **CTS:** The official public CAD ZIP contains 290VBC0F201B2, with the wrong threaded B bushing and 30 mm C0 shaft. This candidate uses only an explicitly qualified shared-family housing subset, retaining its stamped folds and molded carrier. All source terminal solids, below-seat legs, original bushing and original shaft are omitted. The correct unthreaded A bushing and 15 mm A5/F shaft are generated from the manufacturer drawing, retaining shaft tip Z 21.2 mm. This is a manufacturer-housing derivative, not exact BOM manufacturer CAD; CTS rights remain applicable.
- **NKK:** The exact B12AB public CAD preview was configured and inspected. Downloading CAD redirects to sign-in, so no CAD was recovered or access control bypassed. The own parameterized model separates the polymer case, 0.4 mm folded cage with windows, unthreaded bushing and rounded lever. Its 8 × 8.8 × 10.4 mm cage envelope, bushing front Z 13.1 mm and neutral lever tip Z 23.1 mm remain fixed. Unsized windows, molding steps and radii are qualified. The neutral lever is an inspection pose; the actual ON-NONE-ON part would occupy an end position.
- **Grayhill:** The exact-part page states that no 3D model is available. The own model separates the 21.97 mm rounded polymer body, FR4 tongue, double-D bushing, light polymer flatted shaft and integral tab. The full 25.91 mm output-board depth, front datum Z 8.71 mm, bushing projection 6.35 mm and shaft tip Z 27.76 mm are retained. The no-Z part contains no optional metal mounting bracket. Circumferential rings indicate the published 3/8-32 pitch; they are explicitly a visual thread representation, not a true helix or a toleranced thread model.

All three STEP files reload as valid positive-volume geometry and preserve exported volume. Three native KiCad GLB probes reproduce every named feature's palette and canonical bounds: maximum color error 2.92 × 10^-8 and bounds residual 0.000692 mm. STEP color is sRGB; native GLB diffuse factors are linear RGB. STEP does not encode Blender metallic/roughness suggestions.
