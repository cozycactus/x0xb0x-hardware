# x0xb0x parts order list (one unit)

Research only: nothing was ordered and neither repository was changed. Mouser stock and one-piece prices were re-read on **2026-11-05**, before shipping and tax. Re-check them on the day you order.

The CSV is [`x0xb0x-parts-order.csv`](x0xb0x-parts-order.csv). It has one row per BOM line, plus the source URLs and notes columns that this page leaves out. Regenerate both files with `python3 -I scripts/extract_footprints.py <mainboard.kicad_pcb> <ioboard.kicad_pcb>` and then `python3 -I scripts/build_table.py`. The build fails loudly if a reference is missing from the boards, listed twice, or does not add up to the row quantity. It passes for all 112 rows: every populated reference on both KiCad boards appears exactly once.

## Check against the KiCad footprints before ordering

These substitutes change the pitch, body size or pinout, or need a mechanical check:

1. **Film caps (lines 35-43):** 13 positions have holes closer than 5 mm. These are C34, C100 and C101 (3.556 mm), C47 (3.81 mm), and C10, C20, C21, C33, C46, C18, C19, C24, C36 and C201 (4.064 mm). C35 and C45 are on 7.62 mm and need 7.5 mm parts. Only C25, C27, C41, C26 and C54 take 5 mm parts as-is.
2. **SOT-363 chips (lines 61-62):** solder them before any neighbour: 0.65 mm pitch, pin 1 at the silkscreen dot. Fit a chip or its SIP part, never both. The figures here come from illustrative models. R100A's model touches Q126's top corner, reaching 0.09 mm over it, so this depends on the PTC chosen in line 26; keep R100A from pressing on the chip. Q121's land ends 0.07 mm from R110, and Q126's lands 0.07 mm from R100A. C18 reaches 0.31 mm over Q112, 0.41 mm above it, and Q23 reaches 0.5 mm over Q122, 2.09 mm above it.
3. **2SK208 (line 60):** since KiCad revision 30 the mainboard has SC-59 sites for it, Q128 and Q139, routed on the board. Pin 1 is the source, at the silkscreen dot; 2 drain, 3 gate. Take the O grade for Q128 and the Y grade for Q139. Solder them before their neighbours and fit a chip or its TO-92 part, never both. Q128 stands between R105 and C33: in the illustrative models its gate land ends 0.08 mm from R105's body and the chip's body 0.26 mm from it, so stand R105 straight.
4. **Transistors (lines 58-59):** onsemi KSA992 and KSC1815 are 1 emitter, 2 collector, 3 base, leads formed to 2.54 mm, matching the footprint and its flat side. KSC945 only with the **-C** suffix.
5. **9 mm pots (lines 31-32):** Bourns PTV09A has the same pin plan as the original Panasonic EVU: 2.5 mm pins, 7.0 mm behind tabs 10.6 mm apart. The board's holes are 9.144 mm apart, so a tab corner is about 0.2 mm tight, against 0.34 mm for the original: press or nudge the tabs in. The 25 mm shafts have the 450-series knob's 6 mm D flat, and stand 11.5 mm above the panel, as the dual pots do.
6. **Encoder (line 90):** the footprint is named Bourns PEC11, but the CTS 290 drawing fits it: all 7 pins and tabs, smallest margin 0.07 mm. PEC11R is cheaper at Mouser, with 24 detents instead of 20.
7. **78L06 (line 76):** TI UA78L06ACLP is 1 output, 2 common, 3 input, which matches the nets on the IC21 pads.
8. **Trimmers (lines 27-28):** these use Bourns 3362P, because Mouser has no CT-6EP202 or CT-6EP502. Its pin triangle matches the 306 footprint, from the Bourns drawing. Its 6.60 x 6.99 mm body reaches C8's 6.3 mm can by 0.17 mm at TM6, so form C8 0.3 mm away.
9. **Electrolytics:** 5 mm cans have 2.0 mm leads, which must be formed to 2.54 mm (as with XRL). C17 is on 3.556 mm. C3/C5 are 12.5 x 20 mm, with 4.37 mm of headroom under the case and 0.28 mm between them at nominal size, as on the original board.
10. **Resonators (lines 79-80):** Murata CSTLS leads at 2.5 mm are 0.04 mm from the 2.54 mm holes, so they fit.
11. **IC sockets (lines 81-82):** Mill-Max machined sockets sit lower than the 5.1 mm TE ladder socket. This only matters for the chip-top height.
12. **LED spacers (line 57):** Bivar ELM 1-240 is 6.1 mm long with lead slots on 2.54 mm, from its drawing. Keep that length, or the LED height under the panel changes.
13. **PTC (line 26):** an 0805 chip on wire tails across the 10.16 mm pads. Its TC matches the original at 3000 ppm/C, from the TE datasheet.
14. **NYS216-U (line 84):** REAN lists it as the same jack as NYS216, with washer and nut not assembled, so it fits the same footprint.
15. **S3 anchor wire (lines 91 and 99):** S3's holes MT1 and MT4 are on analog ground and MT2 and MT3 on digital. Use two separate wires, MT1-MT4 and MT2-MT3. A diagonal, or one wire through all four holes, joins the two grounds on the mainboard and breaks the star ground. S4's holes are all analog, so its route does not matter. See `../x0xb0x-si-emc/REPORT.md` §6.1.
16. **Optional capacitors (lines 111-114):** these come from the [signal-integrity study](../../x0xb0x-si-emc/REPORT.md), section 1.2, and are not in the original design. They join existing pads, with formed leads. Only the pad spacing was checked, not how they fit. A scope check on the built board decides whether the 100 pF and 1 nF are needed: IC9 pin 8 against IC3 pin 20, and IC9 pin 9 against pin 8, while the sequencer plays rests.
17. **R16 (line 115):** 150 ohm, chosen by the owner on 2026-11-05. The KiCad board's value field and the BOM PDF still say 100 ohm, and the original parts page gives 150 ohm for newer kits. It uses the same pads. The [signal-integrity study](../../x0xb0x-si-emc/REPORT.md), section 12.5, explains the MIDI IN margin; that is a model, so check MIDI IN on the built board.

## Open questions for the owner

- **Q1 R106:** the BOM PDF and the parts page say 24K 1%, but the PCB says 27K. Which value do you want? Both are in the order.
- **Q2 Rare-part strategy:** decided for the 2SC1583 and 2SC2291: SOT-363 chips on the mainboard, KiCad revision 29 (lines 61-62). Decided for the 2SK30A: SC-59 chips, KiCad revision 30 (line 60). Still open for the BA6110: pulls, or the OML clone in IC15A? The LA4140 is Q6.
- **Q3 FT232:** for the Rust firmware test, is USB needed? MIDI plus ISP programming would avoid the scarce FT232BL (about $45-105).
- **Q4 Pots:** settled by the datasheet check: Bourns PTV09A for the five singles and RV112BCF-40-25A-B50K for the B50K dual (lines 31-33). Audio taper replaces the original D, which nobody makes now.
- **Q5 Gain matching:** will you hFE-match the transistors (Q1/Q2, Q3/Q4, the VCF ladder)? If so, buy extras: about 40 NPN, 20 PNP and 10 of each JFET grade.
- **Q6 LA4140:** do you need it for the first bring-up? It only drives the headphone jack.
- **Q7 EEPROM:** the 25LC320A replaces the CAT25C33. Does the firmware's page-write length (and any status-register use) match the 25LC320A datasheet?
- **Q8 Board-to-board jumpers:** the BOM's Digi-Key codes WM09-06 / WM06-06 / WM03-06 could not be resolved. Would 0.1 in ribbon or wire for the 24 conductors be acceptable?
- **Q9 Panels and case:** the panels are settled: FR-4 at a PCB fab, [KiCad project here](../kicad-working/panels/README.md), line 101. Case colour: black, chosen 2026-11-06 (line 100).
- **Q10 Ordering region:** do you order from Mouser US, Farnell UK or LCSC? Stock differs by region. The adapter must be 9 V **AC** for your mains.

## Limits of this check

- Mouser stock and prices on 2026-11-05 cover about 110 part numbers, read in a browser session with no bot check.
- On 2026-10-17 Mouser, Digi-Key, TME and later Farnell put up bot checks or CAPTCHAs. I did not try to get around them.
- Generic hardware was not checked. The substitutes in lines 26-28, 31-33, 35-39, 41, 43, 44, 57 and 95 were compared on paper with their datasheets or Mouser's listed dimensions and with the board's footprints on 2026-11-05. Nothing has been fitted. Knob bore depth was not found.
- Digi-Key numbers in the original BOM were not re-checked.
- LCSC "Other Suppliers" offers are marketplace stock with long lead times.
- "In stock" is distributor stock on 2026-11-05, not a guarantee. LCSC, Digi-Key and Farnell figures that remain are older.
